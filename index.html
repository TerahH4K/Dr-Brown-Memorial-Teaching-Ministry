Our History

From Torch Bearers to Star Bearers

Six decades of ministry, carried forward by two generations of leadership.

Dr. Herbert Alfred Brown

Founder · Anthropologist & Missionary, London Missionary Society

In the early 1960s, Dr. Brown founded a movement called Torch Bearers of Papua, referenced in hymn 170 of the 1969 Toaripi-language hymn book, Fara Aea Veveu.

His vision was for the beginners of Papua to bear and carry the light of the Gospel. For decades the movement operated informally within the United Church, which limited its access to government and development support.

Andrew Lauari Siari

Founder · Retired High School Teacher, B.A. Philosophy (UPNG)

A co-worker of Dr. Brown, Mr. Siari was inspired to reform the movement — renaming Torch Bearers as Star Bearers — with a new and lasting vision.

His vision: "The development of the soul and support services to excel in life." Under his leadership the ministry became a formally registered Faith-Based Organization.

Our Vision

The development of soul through education, and support services to build and strengthen sustainable living.

Our Mission

Five goals, in step with the United Church

  • Teach beginners the word of God and develop the soul.

  • Raise young men and women to carry the banner of Jesus and shine the light of the Gospel as Star Bearers.

  • Initiate and provide support services to the community for sustainable living.

  • Equip members with trade skills for job opportunities.

  • Serve as a development agent for our partner organizations.

Why a star?

Our emblem is the Star. In Revelation 22:16, Jesus speaks of Himself as the "bright morning star." The star is therefore a banner of Jesus — and His followers are called to wear and carry His name as Star Bearers.
